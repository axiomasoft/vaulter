PostgreSQL review: SELECT ... ORDER BY ... LIMIT 1000 FOR UPDATE locks the selected rows until the re-arm commits. A competing tick waits and then re-evaluates eligibility. A failing save rolls back the entire claim before any dispatch. A lost queued job becomes due after the claim window. These properties were checked by package tests plus a separate two-process barrier probe; they are distinct from the P6 product gate.

**Implementation Rules:** Profile begins with run validity and distinguishes `php_allocator_peak`,
`VmHWM`, `Pss_Anon`, `Pss_File`, cgroup `anon/file/sock`; no column is relabelled RSS. Every cell
links three repetitions and reports min/median/max plus fixed ceiling verdict. `bounded-at-envelope`
requires complete upload bytes/checksum or, for download, complete HTTP/`Content-Length`/isolated
`data_received` plus separate server/object checksum, no OOM/restart, and all size cells under the
predeclared ceiling. It never claims a client payload checksum while `responseType:none` is active.
L-01 closes only when `s3_complete_postfix/5GiB` and its smaller cells are GREEN `<160 MiB`; otherwise
the existing L-01 stays open and P4.4 cannot close GREEN. New debt rows use `L-P4-nn`, severity,
confidence, path/size, affected SLO, exact evidence ids, owner package, candidate dev-slice and D11.
File/cache-only growth is reported as I/O footprint, not heap leak. All product verdicts are GREEN;
the only admitted product-source read is the CAS stage anchor needed to classify its repeatable
5 GiB `memory.events.max` pressure without treating it as a PHP-memory regression.
**Code Guidance:** Three sizes establish envelope evidence, not asymptotic proof; use “observed”,
“bounded at tested envelope”, “size-correlated” or “inconclusive”. Do not average away OOM or take
the best repetition. Preserve D29 pre-fix evidence as baseline history but do not mix it into the
post-fix run family. Real AWS completion semantics are context only; Vaulter proxied path owns verdict.
**Validation:** profile validator confirms 15/15 cells ×3 reps and all raw evidence links; fixed
ceiling map is byte-identical to P4.2 manifest; L-01 check requires GREEN 5 GiB complete `<160 MiB`;
every RED/inconclusive non-L01 cell maps to one debt/limitation; Markdown/path check; no
`packages/*` edits in P4 diff; plan-lint/downstream checks green.
**Deliverables:** `memory-profile.md` as authoritative P4 verdict and
`findings/P4-memory-profile.md` as bounded remediation backlog with explicit L-01 outcome.
